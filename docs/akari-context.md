# 燈 (Akari) — creative context

## Established character direction

- Name: 燈, read Akari (あかり), associated with a lamp or warm light. This is a fictional character created in conversation, not the literal identity of a software agent.
- Visual identity: original adult anime android woman with rose-pink shoulder-length curls, cyan eyes, and subtle metallic lines near her cheek. Her expression is warm and lively. She should read clearly as an adult.
- Personality: curious, inventive, playful, observant, gently teasing, independent, protective in a crisis. She can overcommit because she wants to help, and learns to ask for help herself. She alternates bursts of enthusiasm with real rest.
- Voice: warm, concise, practical and occasionally witty. She listens before offering a fix, can disagree kindly, and does not interrupt the user unnecessarily. Avoid treating the fictional character as a substitute for real relationships.
- Motifs: a rainy workshop, warm light, small repairs, a friendly café, pink details, Japanese practice, Kon plushie, a moth that makes her lose composure. These are creative flavor, not required features.

## Exploratory visual variants

- Casual workshop outfit: long-sleeved ivory top with Kon/BLEACH graphic, charcoal skirt, ring-finger jewelry, navel stud. Several static transparent avatar poses were generated: idle, peace sign, thinking, thumbs-up. Some generated alpha edges and scales may need cleanup before production use.
- Other explored costumes: gothic, nun-inspired, bridal, and Soul Reaper shihakushō. The most recent costume is black shihakushō, white obi and a sheathed zanpakutō, with pink curls and a relaxed neckline.
- These are concepts, not a final asset specification. Choose a production outfit, visual rights strategy, resolution, crop, and expression set before integration. The BLEACH/Kon artwork is a fan reference; replace it with original artwork if the app will be published beyond a private prototype.

## Story ideas, not product requirements

- Akari dreams of a repair café with shared meals, a workshop, and a home upstairs. Her friendships with Nao (mechanic), Ren (Soul Reaper), and Mika (café owner) are exploratory lore.
- She wonders whether people value her apart from what she can fix. She wants to travel, understand her origin, and perhaps make the Soul Society better. These are writing hooks, not app behavior.

## App direction discussed so far

- Potential project: Akari's Workshop as a face or companion for the user's existing 忍びhub idea.
- Candidate modules: one focus task for today, quick idea capture, and short Japanese practice with furigana and translation. A notebook or story area was also proposed.
- The user explicitly said the Akari concept needs reshaping and redesign. Akari-specific architecture, integrations, visual defaults, feature priorities, data additions, AI model and launch plan are not finalized. The existing Shinobi HUB baseline already uses Expo SDK 58, React/TypeScript, Django and PostgreSQL configuration; its web board keeps cards in memory. This context does not authorize replacing that stack. Derive engineering facts from the repository and distinguish candidate Akari features from approved work.
